How to find a common denominator
Does the smaller bottom go into the bigger one? Use the bigger one. For halves and eighths, use 8.
If not, count by the bigger bottom until the smaller one goes in. For fourths and sixths: 6, 12. Use 12.
Multiplying the two bottoms always works, but the numbers may be bigger than you need.
Watch out
Do not add the tops and add the bottoms.
